01 / Thesis

Governments simulate after the decision. Markets simulate before.

The asymmetry is the whole problem. A trading desk models a position a thousand ways before it ships; a ministry ships policy and discovers the consequences in the press. Sovereign Twin imports the market's discipline into the sovereign's office.

See reality

Perception with provenance

Every input to the twin carries a source and a timestamp. No asserted states — a claim without provenance is treated as noise, the same law that governs KONTOR's markets.

Read consequences

Counterfactuals, graded

Decisions are simulated across horizon ladders: 30 days, 6 months, 3 years. Each consequence carries its confidence and the assumptions it dies with.

Move first

Options, not answers

The twin's output is a ranked option set with trigger conditions — built for principals who must commit before certainty exists.

02 / System

One twin, three disciplines.

The concept stands on engineering law already proven in the portfolio — nothing here is invented from scratch.

Sensing

Sovereign Grid's market-intel engine (indicative → quoted → transacted) generalized to sovereign data: every number in the twin is a price, a measurement, or a document — never a vibe.

Honesty

Alleadz's claim law: cite or label it a hypothesis. The twin's briefs are audit artifacts — a minister can ask "why does the model believe this" and receive an evidence chain, not a shrug.

Agency

KONTOR's mandate law: the twin advises; it never acts. Options carry dual-signature gates. The machine that models the state does not hold the state's pen.

Honest status: this is a concept — a designed thesis, not a product. No code exists yet. The design law above is inherited from systems that do exist and run: Sovereign Grid (live), Alleadz (alpha), KONTOR (on-chain E2E). The next step is a paid-spec engagement, not a promise.
