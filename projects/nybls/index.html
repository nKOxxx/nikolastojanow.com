Nybls

Paranormal levels of curiosity, fed. Nybls hunts down the signal inside YouTube videos, podcasts, articles, PDFs and courses — then turns each source into an interactive learning session: a brief, key moments you can jump into, and quizzes that prove you actually understood it. Not another summary. Knowledge you can remember and use.

01 / The product

Find it. Watch it. Learn it. Use it.

Nybls absorbs the source so you don't have to sit through all of it — then makes sure it sticks.

Signal

Hunts the signal inside the noise

Feed it a link — a talk, a podcast episode, a paper. Nybls absorbs the source, maps the key ideas and finds what's actually worth your time.

Structure

Briefs and mental models

Every source becomes "the idea in 60 seconds" plus a structured brief — the concepts, the order to learn them in, and why they connect.

Moments

Jump straight to key moments

Timestamped highlights let you drop into the exact part of the video or document that matters — proof, context, or the quote you needed.

Proof

Quizzes that prove understanding

Sessions end with "prove you understand" checks and ways to put the knowledge to work — because consuming isn't learning.

02 / How it works

Four steps, every source.

One loop, from raw link to working knowledge.

Capturegive Nybls a YouTube, podcast, article, PDF or course link
Mapthe source is absorbed and key ideas are extracted
Learnwork the brief, jump through key moments, ask questions
Usequiz yourself, then apply it — insight into action
03 / Under the hood

Born from an evidence pipeline.

The discipline underneath the friendly surface.

Extraction

Grounded in the source, always

Nybls grew out of an agentic harvest pipeline — transcripts, frame sampling and timestamped receipts. Every claim in a session traces back to the source material.

Agents

Built with, and for, agents

The harvest engine is scriptable end to end: any agent with a shell can capture, probe and study sources through the same core that powers the site.

Open access

No gate, no account

The site is public — visitors try the full learning experience signed-out. Curiosity shouldn't start with a sign-up form.

Direction

Where it goes next

Deeper session interactivity, more source types, and shared finds — a library of distilled sources people can browse and learn from.
