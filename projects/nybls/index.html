The idea in 60 seconds

An AI agent is a loop, not a chatbot.

The model reasons about a goal, chooses a tool, observes what happened, and adjusts. The difference is not intelligence alone. It is the ability to take actions, inspect their consequences, and continue until the goal is complete.

A clear outcome and boundaries define what the agent is allowed to pursue.

Explain the agent loop like I run a restaurant.
Think of the agent as a head waiter. You give it the goal: keep every table served. It checks the room, assigns a task, sees what changed, and adjusts. It does not create one perfect plan at opening time. It keeps observing and acting throughout service.
