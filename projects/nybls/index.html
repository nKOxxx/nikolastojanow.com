Nybls turns videos, podcasts, articles, PDFs and courses into interactive learning sessions — briefs, key moments, quizzes. It doesn't trust captions: it looks at the frames, and shows you what it looked at.

Look first, trust later.

pipeline — transcript is the floor, not the ceiling

01 · probe

Cheap eyes first

Low-res frame probes run on a budget of zero vision calls. Change scores find where the video actually does something.

02 · look

Spend where it matters

High-res tile passes only on the kept frames — every vision unit is accounted for and printed in the receipt.

03 · distill

Session, not summary

Brief, key moments with timestamps, quiz pairs. The output is something you study, not something you skim.

04 · prove

Show the evidence

Frame sheets ship with the session. Claim a moment? The screenshot of that moment is right there.
