Nybls

A watching layer for video knowledge. Nybls captures long-form video and livestreams, transcribes them, samples the visual track, and distills hours of footage into structured, timestamped intelligence — blueprints, briefings and quotes you can cite. Search engines index text on a page; Nybls indexes what was actually said and shown.

01 / The product

Watch everything. Cite the moment.

Built for people who need to know what hundreds of hours of video actually contain.

Capture

Long-form in, lean archive out

Livestreams, panels, keynotes and course content are auto-captured into lean text + frame archives — the raw video is harvested and released, never hoarded.

Transcribe

Word-level transcripts

Speech-to-text tuned for long sessions, timecoded end to end so every statement can be traced back to the second it was said.

Distill

Blueprints, not summaries

Multi-day archives are distilled into structured blueprints: the claims, the numbers, the slides, the quotes — each linked to the moment it appeared.

Receipts

Evidence, on demand

Every distilled statement can be opened to its timestamp and frame. No hallucinated citations, no "trust me" — the moment itself is the source.

02 / The pipeline

Five stages, one discipline.

Every stage feeds the next; nothing reaches the output without a receipt.

03 / Principles

Built like an archive, not an app.

The unglamorous rules that make the output citable.

Evidence rule

No claim without a moment

Distillation is extraction, not generation. If a statement can't be traced to a timestamp in the source, it doesn't ship.

Budget rule

Transcript first, frames on target

Text carries the bulk of the signal; frame analysis is spent surgically where visuals matter — slides, charts, demos.

Local-first

Own the pipeline

Capture, transcription and sampling run on owned hardware with open models — no vendor can see the corpus or cut the cord.

Private beta

Gated while it sharpens

Nybls runs behind login while the distillation quality is tuned. Output ships as documents: blueprints, briefings, threads and post drafts.
