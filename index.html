05 / Biography

From protocol founder to institutional systems builder.

This section is designed to become the canonical short bio for press, speaking pages and intros.

Nikola Stojanow is an entrepreneur, venture builder and frontier systems architect working across AI, governance, digital infrastructure and digital assets. He is Partner, New Ventures at Bank Frick, where his work sits at the intersection of regulated banking, institutional crypto infrastructure and new financial rails.

He co-founded æternity, an early Layer-1 blockchain network, and later led the Aeternity Foundation through ecosystem growth, strategic partnerships and infrastructure initiatives including Hyperchains. Through AE Ventures and Starfleet, he helped build and fund blockchain companies across several markets.

His venture background includes Vitosha Venture Partners, Meta Change Capital, Techstars mentoring and advisory work across blockchain, AI, capital formation and institutional transformation. Today his broader product and research work focuses on systems that help organizations see earlier, decide better and build infrastructure for a more autonomous operating environment.

“The question is not whether systems will change. The question is whether institutions can see the change early enough to act.”
